Search your maps the way osu! does, play the songs, see what's in a chart and keep the library tidy. Fumen reads what osu!lazer already has, and never changes a thing behind its back.

What it does

Find any map in a second

Search the way osu! does: keys=7, sr>4.5, status=loved, played=no. Filters and star ranges are a click away, and the list stays smooth at twenty thousand difficulties.

Know a chart before you play it

Every difficulty is read once: notes per second, the patterns in it, the hardest part and, for osu!mania, an estimated dan. Watch it on the playfield at any rate, with your own skin.

Add a whole pile at once

Paste every link from a pool or a thread, or let Fumen find each map you've played that osu!lazer doesn't have. The sets come from the mirrors and go into osu!lazer the way it imports them itself.

Take everything a search finds

Browse osu!'s beatmaps through the mirrors and play the previews. Add one set, or every new one a search turns up: search miku and all the sets you don't have yet go into osu!lazer in one go.

Keep the library tidy

Duplicates, broken files and what takes the space. Deleting goes through osu!lazer's own Delete, and your scores stay.
